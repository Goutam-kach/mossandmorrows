## Fonts

- Fraunces variable Latin WOFF2: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/fraunces:vf@latest/latin-wght-normal.woff2
- Manrope variable Latin WOFF2: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/fontsource/fonts/manrope:vf@latest/latin-wght-normal.woff2
- Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Complete licenses are in `FRAUNCES-LICENSE.txt` and `MANROPE-LICENSE.txt`.
- The downloaded font files are committed locally; the site never calls the above endpoints at runtime.

## Libraries and Icons

- Three.js 0.160.1: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.160.1/build/three.min.js
- Lucide 0.468.0: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide@0.468.0/dist/umd/lucide.min.js
- Complete license notices are preserved under `../vendor/`.
- The two yarn balls, hooks, color-matched yarn strands, crochet planter, textured botanical centerpiece, and fully stitched amigurumi birds are procedural Three.js geometry. Crochet bump texture is generated locally in the browser. No external 3D models or textures are required.
